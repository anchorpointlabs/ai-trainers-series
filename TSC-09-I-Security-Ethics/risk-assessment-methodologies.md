# Risk assessment methodologies

> **TSC #9 Generative AI Models — Technical Aspects of Security and Ethics · Intermediate · Knowledge K3**

**IMDA requirement:** Risk assessment methodologies.

**What it means:** A risk assessment is a structured look at what could go wrong, how likely, and how bad. Methodologies — like a risk matrix (likelihood × impact) or bowtie analysis (causes → event → consequences) — make the assessment repeatable instead of a gut feel.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Use a risk matrix to rank risks.
- Explain a bowtie analysis in one line.
- Run a simple assessment on a scenario.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Teach the risk matrix (plot likelihood vs impact). 
2. Show a bowtie (causes on the left, consequences on the right). 
3. Learners rank three risks on a matrix. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Rank these on a likelihood×impact matrix: (a) model hallucinates a price, (b) model leaks a name, (c) model is rude to a customer.
```
Expected: learners place each and justify the position.

**Common mistakes:**
- Assessing risk by gut feel instead of a matrix.
- Ranking everything 'high' and acting on nothing.
