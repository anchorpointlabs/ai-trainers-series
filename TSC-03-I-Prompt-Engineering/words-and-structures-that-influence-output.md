# Words and structures that influence output

> **TSC #3 Prompt Engineering · Intermediate · Knowledge K3**

**Bullet (verbatim):** Use of words and structures through prompts to influence AI outputs (e.g., multi-lingual prompting, multi-turn prompting for conversations).

**What it means:** The words you choose and how you structure the exchange steer the output. **Word choice** sets tone and register ("summarise" vs "critique"). **Multi-lingual prompting** asks for output in another language. **Multi-turn prompting** builds context step by step instead of dumping everything at once.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Steer tone with word choice.
- Prompt for an output in another language.
- Use multi-turn to build context across several messages.

**How to teach it in 20 minutes:**
1. Show one task prompted three ways with visibly different tones. (5 min)
2. Teach multi-lingual and multi-turn with a customer-service example. (5 min)
3. Learners reword a prompt to shift its tone, and write a multi-turn exchange. (7 min)
4. Compare results. (3 min)

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Turn 1: "List three ways to reduce energy use in an office."
Turn 2 (new message, same chat): "Now phrase those three for a staff notice in Singlish, friendly tone."
```
Expected: the second turn reuses the list and changes register.

**Common mistakes:**
- Treating word choice as trivial.
- Dumping a complex brief into one turn instead of building it step by step.
