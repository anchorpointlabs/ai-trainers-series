# Advocate for responsible AI development

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Advanced · Ability A10**

**IMDA requirement:** Advocate for responsible and ethical development and use of generative AI technologies, including user privacy rights.

**What it means:** Advocacy at this level is organisational and public: making the case for privacy rights, for responsible deployment, and for the cost of getting it wrong — with evidence and a constructive path, not alarm.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Make a case for privacy rights.
- Use evidence to argue for responsibility.
- Propose a constructive path forward.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Contrast alarm ('AI is dangerous') with advocacy ('here's the risk, the evidence, and the fix'). 
2. Teach 'advocacy = evidence + a path'. 
3. Learners draft a short advocacy statement. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Draft a two-sentence advocacy statement for user privacy in a new AI feature, with evidence and a path.
```
Expected: a risk (data exposure), evidence (a past breach), and a path (privacy-preserving design).

**Common mistakes:**
- Advocating with alarm and no evidence.
- No constructive path, so the argument stalls.
