# Apply comprehensive ethical frameworks

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Advanced · Ability A1**

**IMDA requirement:** Apply comprehensive ethical frameworks to AI projects.

**What it means:** A comprehensive framework covers the full lifecycle and all four lenses — and applying it means using it to make real decisions at each stage, with owners and evidence, not just citing it.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Apply a framework across a lifecycle.
- Produce a decision with an owner.
- Name what 'comprehensive' must include.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Walk a comprehensive framework across a project. 
2. Teach 'owner + evidence = applied'. 
3. Learners apply the framework to one stage. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Apply a fairness framework to a loan model's evaluation stage: what do you check, and who owns it?
```
Expected: fairness metrics across groups, owned by a named person with sign-off.

**Common mistakes:**
- Citing a framework without applying it.
- No owner, so no one is accountable.
