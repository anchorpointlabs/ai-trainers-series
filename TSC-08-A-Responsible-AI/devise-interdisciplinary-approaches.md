# Devise interdisciplinary approaches

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Advanced · Ability A8**

**IMDA requirement:** Devise interdisciplinary approaches to address ethical challenges in AI, including moderating AI-generated content as required.

**What it means:** Hard ethical problems need more than one discipline — a lawyer, a designer, a community member, an engineer working together. Devising an approach means assembling the right mix and producing a solution that respects all of them.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Assemble an interdisciplinary team for a problem.
- Explain why one discipline is insufficient.
- Propose a cross-disciplinary solution.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Show a moderation problem that needs law + design + community. 
2. Teach 'the hard problems are not single-discipline'. 
3. Learners assemble a team for a scenario. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
For moderating AI-generated content on a community platform, which three disciplines do you involve, and why?
```
Expected: law (what's illegal), design (what's usable), community (what's acceptable locally).

**Common mistakes:**
- Solving a multi-discipline problem with one lens.
- Assembling a team with no community voice.
