# Analyse ethical dilemmas in governance and regulation

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Advanced · Ability A5**

**Bullet (verbatim):** Analyse ethical dilemmas in AI governance, regulation, and global contexts.

**What it means:** Governance dilemmas are trade-offs with no clean answer — privacy vs security, innovation vs precaution, global vs local rules. Analysing them means laying out both sides and the stakeholders, then arguing a defensible position.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Lay out both sides of a dilemma.
- Name the stakeholders.
- Argue a defensible position.

**How to teach it in 20 minutes:**
1. Present a dilemma (privacy vs security in AI surveillance). (5 min)
2. Teach 'a dilemma is two goods in tension'. (5 min)
3. Learners argue both sides of a dilemma. (7 min)
4. Debrief. (3 min)

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Argue both sides: a city wants AI to scan public messages for threats. Then state your position.
```
Expected: security (prevent harm) vs privacy (mass surveillance), then a reasoned stance.

**Common mistakes:**
- Pretending a dilemma has one obviously right answer.
- Arguing a side without naming the stakeholders.
