# Ethical frameworks and global standards

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Advanced · Knowledge K1**

**Bullet (verbatim):** Ethical frameworks and guidelines relevant to AI development (including global standards or frameworks for data privacy).

**What it means:** Global standards — like the OECD AI principles, the EU AI Act, and international privacy frameworks — set the norms that a serious AI programme aligns with. The skill is knowing which standard applies to your context and what it demands.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Name the key global standards.
- Match a standard to a context.
- State what one standard demands.

**How to teach it in 20 minutes:**
1. Map the standards (OECD, EU AI Act, ISO) to what each governs. (7 min)
2. Teach 'the standard you must meet depends on your market'. (5 min)
3. Learners pick the relevant standard for a scenario. (5 min)
4. Debrief. (3 min)

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Your AI product will serve EU customers. Which standard should you study first, and why?
```
Expected: the EU AI Act — it directly regulates AI in the EU market.

**Common mistakes:**
- Applying a standard without checking it fits the market.
- Ignoring global standards for a local-only product that may scale.
