# TSC #7 Generative AI Model Development and Fine Tuning — Advanced

> **Levels:** B · I · A
> **Deep dive:** [`genai-image.md`](../core-knowledge/genai-image.md)

**What this skill is:** Design, implement, train, and evaluate generative models — deep learning, training data, fine-tuning, deployment.

**Descriptor (Advanced):** Design novel architectures and algorithms; mitigate risk and ethical concerns.

**Knowledge:**
- Advanced theories; hardware libraries (CUDA); research frontiers.
- Specialised uses (protein, 3D, world models); compute efficiency; synthetic data.
- Advanced fine-tuning and alignment (RLHF, RLAIF).

**Abilities:**
- Devise novel methodologies; lead projects; optimise.
- Architect large-scale pre-processing; design agentic and neuro-symbolic systems; independent research.

**Teach it with:**
**The model's education:** *pre-training* = general schooling (read/write/reason); *fine-tuning* = a specialist course on your domain; *training data* = the textbook — garbage in, garbage out. GANs are 'a forger and a detective training each other'; a VAE compresses a picture into a short code then rebuilds it; a transformer reads the whole sentence at once to decide the next word.
