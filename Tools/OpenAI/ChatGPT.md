# ChatGPT — OpenAI

**Tier:** Living · **As at:** 26 November 2026 · **Expires:** versions and pricing rot within a quarter.

**What it is:** A general-purpose AI assistant for writing, analysis, learning, research, and work; useful to individuals and teams, not a guarantee of correct answers.

## The product

ChatGPT is the application people use to converse with OpenAI models and, depending on their account and settings, work with files, images, voice, and other tools. It is not one fixed model: the product experience depends on the selected model, available tools, plan, workspace settings, and usage limits. For a business owner, the useful question is usually **“Can I give it the right context and check its work?”**, not “Which model name sounds strongest?”

## Current line-up (November 2026)

**This page does not carry a verified November 2026 public line-up.** The names **GPT-6 Sol, Astra, Luna, 6.1 Sol, 6.1 Terra and Cyber** have not been checked against OpenAI's own documentation, so their public availability, capabilities, release dates, and which plans include them are unconfirmed. Do not put them on a learner handout as a confirmed line-up.

The durable distinction is between the **ChatGPT product**, its **subscription plans**, and the **models and tools available inside a particular account**. Historically documented plans include Free, Plus, and Pro; organisational offerings have also existed. Their November 2026 names, entitlements, model limits, and regional availability require a live account-and-pricing check. A trainer should capture a dated screenshot of the actual model picker before teaching a model-selection exercise.

## What it's best at / not best at

ChatGPT is a strong starting point for turning rough ideas into a structured draft, explaining an unfamiliar concept, comparing options, rehearsing a conversation, and analysing material the learner provides. It is particularly useful when a person can inspect and improve the result over several turns.

It is **not** the authority for a company’s current price, a client’s contractual terms, Singapore legal compliance, or a fact it cannot source. It can produce confident but unsupported claims. It may also miss a qualification hidden in a long document or misunderstand what the user is authorised to do with uploaded data.

**Trainer’s decision rule:** use ChatGPT when the work benefits from drafting or reasoning **and a human can verify the consequential parts**. For a binding quotation, sensitive personal data, regulated decision, or external action, first establish the source of truth, permitted data use, and approval step.

## Pricing (November 2026)

**November 2026 prices are unverified.** OpenAI announced ChatGPT Plus at **US$20/month in February 2023** and ChatGPT Pro at **US$200/month in December 2024**. Those are **historical launch prices, not a November 2026 price list**. Free access has historically existed, but its model access and limits can change.

Before class, check OpenAI’s current pricing page for the learner’s country, billing currency, taxes, usage limits, and whether a feature shown in a demonstration is included in the relevant plan. Do not tell a Singapore learner that a US-dollar list price is their final Singapore-dollar charge.

## What this actually teaches

**Knowledge is not the same as fluent output.** ChatGPT is a good vehicle for teaching learners to supply relevant business context, ask for an explicit assumption list, inspect claims, and decide what needs human approval. In Anchor Point Labs terms, improving the *knowledge* supplied to an agent does not remove the need for human ability to judge and verify its work.

## The student lens

**Mental model:** A fast colleague at a whiteboard: helpful at organising ideas, but not automatically briefed on your company or empowered to sign off its answers.

**Misconceptions:**
- “It sounds certain, so it checked.” **Correction:** confidence of wording is not evidence; request sources and verify important claims.
- “It remembers our whole business.” **Correction:** available context varies by chat, product settings, and workspace. Provide or connect the approved source of truth.
- “The newest model is always the right choice.” **Correction:** compare the task, tools, speed, limits, data requirements, and quality of the output.

## The adult educator lens

**Analogy:** A new employee in a Singapore SME who writes a polished customer reply after hearing half a briefing. The analogy breaks because ChatGPT has no employee’s ongoing accountability, workplace awareness, or inherent authority to act.

**Three-minute demo:** Give it an underspecified request: “Reply to a customer asking for our price and delivery date.” Show the unsupported assumptions. Then supply a *fictional, approved* price table, service scope, and instruction to flag unknown delivery dates. Compare the drafts.

**Where it goes wrong in class:** The trainer uses real client details or an outdated price, then praises the smoother second answer without checking whether it is true.

**Check for understanding:** “Which sentence in this draft can you verify from the supplied company facts, and which sentence needs a person to check?”

## Watch out

Retire claims that ChatGPT is one model, that a named model is available to every subscriber, or that a plan price remembered from launch is current. Verify the model picker, plan entitlements, data controls, and local billing immediately before class. **Do not present the requested GPT-6 family names as a verified public product catalogue on the strength of this runtime’s identity.**
