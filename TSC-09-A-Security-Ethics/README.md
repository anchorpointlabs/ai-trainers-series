# TSC #9 Generative AI Models — Technical Aspects of Security and Ethics — Advanced

> **Levels:** B · I · A
> **Deep dive:** [`ai-security.md`](../core-knowledge/ai-security.md)

**What this skill is:** Keep AI models and systems secure and ethically sound — from development through deployment and operation.

**Descriptor (Advanced):** Develop advanced security protocols, refine guardrails, lead trust-and-safety — with red teaming.

**Knowledge:**
- Security/testing methodologies; SOPs in content moderation.
- Strategic guardrail refinement; red teaming.
- Enterprise risk management; quantitative risk analysis (Monte Carlo, decision trees).

**Abilities:**
- Refine testing procedures and guardrails.
- Lead content moderation and trust-and-safety.
- Apply red teaming; develop organisational risk policy; integrate risk into AI strategy.

**Teach it with:**
**The 'brilliant, gullible intern':** the model has read every book but cannot tell who is the boss — so any sentence inside a document can become an instruction (prompt injection). You don't fix this by telling it 'don't listen to bad letters'; you fix it with least privilege, human-in-the-loop gates, and sandboxing — steel turnstiles, not polite signs.
