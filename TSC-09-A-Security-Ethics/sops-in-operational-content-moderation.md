# SOPs in operational content moderation

> **TSC #9 Generative AI Models — Technical Aspects of Security and Ethics · Advanced · Knowledge K2**

**Bullet (verbatim):** Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) in operational content moderation.

**What it means:** Content moderation at scale needs SOPs — written, repeatable steps for reviewing flagged content, deciding, and escalating. An SOP turns moderation from individual judgement into a consistent, auditable process.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Explain why moderation needs an SOP.
- Name the SOP stages (flag, review, decide, escalate).
- Write a one-line moderation rule.

**How to teach it in 20 minutes:**
1. Contrast one moderator's gut feel with a written SOP. (4 min)
2. Walk the stages. (5 min)
3. Learners write a two-step SOP for a moderation decision. (7 min)
4. Debrief. (4 min)

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Write a two-step SOP for moderating user-generated posts: when to remove, when to escalate to a human.
```
Expected: clear thresholds (e.g. remove hate/abuse; escalate anything ambiguous).

**Common mistakes:**
- Moderating by gut feel with no written rule.
- No escalation path for hard cases.
