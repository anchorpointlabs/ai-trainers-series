# Evaluate reliability and adaptability

> **TSC #4 Generative AI Model Selection · Advanced · Ability A3**

**IMDA requirement:** Evaluate the reliability and adaptability of advanced generative models for real-world applications.

**What it means:** Reliability = consistent, correct behaviour over time; adaptability = the model handles new inputs and domains without breaking. Both are evaluated by stressing the model over a sustained period and varied inputs, not by a single impressive demo.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Distinguish reliability from adaptability.
- Design a stress test for a model.
- State what 'consistent over time' means.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Contrast a flashy demo with steady, boring reliability. 
2. Teach the two axes (reliability, adaptability). 
3. Learners design a week-long stress test. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Design a stress test for a chatbot: what inputs and time window would you use to judge reliability and adaptability?
```
Expected: a varied input set over time, checking for drift and edge-case failures.

**Common mistakes:**
- Judging a model on one impressive demo.
- Confusing adaptability with reliability.
