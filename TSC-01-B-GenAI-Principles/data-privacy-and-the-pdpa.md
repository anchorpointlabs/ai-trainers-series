# Data privacy and the PDPA

> **TSC #1 Generative AI Principles and Applications · Basic · Knowledge K8**

**IMDA requirement:** Fundamentals of data privacy and protection (e.g., data privacy laws and regulations such as Personal Data Protection Act).

**What it means:** Personal data — name, NRIC, contact, health — is protected by Singapore's **PDPA**: get consent, use it only for the stated purpose, and keep it safe. The practical rule for GenAI: **never paste customer or staff personal data into a public AI tool**, because you may be disclosing it to a third party without consent.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Identify what counts as personal data.
- State the three PDPA basics (consent, purpose, protection).
- Decide whether a piece of data may be entered into a tool.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Ask: what would you not want on the office noticeboard? That is the test.
2. Teach consent, purpose, protection with a customer-email example.
3. Learners classify ten items (name, job title, salary, company name, NRIC, email).
4. Debrief: which were personal, which were not obvious.

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
For each item, reply 'personal data' or 'not personal data':
1. Tan Mei Ling, 35
2. S1234567A
3. Customer Service Manager
4. mei.ling@example.com
```
Expected: 1, 2 and 4 are personal data (name, NRIC, email); 3 alone is not.

**Common mistakes:**
- Pasting customer details into a public chatbot.
- Assuming a job title is not personal data (it can identify someone when combined).
- Assuming anonymised data is always safe.
