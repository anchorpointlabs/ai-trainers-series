# Analyse ethical implications of AI algorithms

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Intermediate · Ability A5**

**IMDA requirement:** Analyse the ethical implications of AI algorithms (e.g., assess bias and fairness concerns).

**What it means:** Analysing implications means looking past 'does it work' to 'who does it treat unfairly, and how could this go wrong'. It is a structured look at bias and fairness, not a gut reaction.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Assess a model for bias.
- Name who could be treated unfairly.
- Describe a downstream harm.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Walk an analysis on a real-sounding model (loan, hiring). 
2. Teach the two questions (who is treated unfairly, how it goes wrong). 
3. Learners analyse a model for fairness. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
Analyse a resume-screener that learned to prefer CVs with 'male' hobbies. Who is treated unfairly, and what harm results?
```
Expected: women are unfairly filtered, and the harm is lost opportunity at scale.

**Common mistakes:**
- Stopping at 'it works' without asking who it hurts.
- Analysing bias with no concrete affected group in mind.
