# Ethical considerations — bias, privacy, transparency, accountability

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Intermediate · Knowledge K1**

**IMDA requirement:** Ethical considerations in AI development, (e.g., bias, privacy, transparency, accountability).

**What it means:** For a developer, the four considerations become design decisions: bias (what the training data encodes), privacy (what data the system touches), transparency (how explainable it is), and accountability (who answers when it fails). Each is engineered, not just intended.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Name the four considerations.
- Relate each to a design decision.
- Explain why bias lives in the training data.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Walk one model's bias back to its training data. 
2. Teach the four as design decisions. 
3. Learners name the consideration behind a design choice. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
A hiring model was trained on ten years of past hires (mostly male engineers). Which consideration is at risk, and why?
```
Expected: bias — the training data encodes the historical skew.

**Common mistakes:**
- Treating the four as slogans rather than design decisions.
- Blaming the model for bias it inherited from data.
