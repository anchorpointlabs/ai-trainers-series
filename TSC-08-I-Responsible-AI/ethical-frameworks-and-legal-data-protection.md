# Ethical frameworks and legal data protection

> **TSC #8 Responsible AI and Generative AI Practices · Intermediate · Knowledge K5**

**IMDA requirement:** Ethical frameworks and guidelines for generative AI-powered tools (including legal requirements for data protection).

**What it means:** Frameworks give a structure for the four considerations, and the law gives the floor — in Singapore, PDPA sets the data-protection minimum. The skill is operating above the legal floor, using the framework as the guide.

**The learner must be able to:**
- Name a framework and its structure.
- State the legal floor in Singapore (PDPA).
- Explain 'legal floor vs ethical ceiling'.

**Lesson Plan Ideas:**
1. Teach the floor-vs-ceiling idea: law is the minimum, ethics is the standard. 
2. Map a framework (principles → controls) onto a tool. 
3. Learners state what PDPA requires for a use case. 
4. Debrief. 

**Exercise (with the exact prompt):**
```text
For a tool that stores customer data, what does PDPA require as the floor, and what would ethics add on top?
```
Expected: PDPA (consent, purpose, protection); ethics adds transparency and fairness.

**Common mistakes:**
- Treating legal compliance as the end of the story.
- Using a framework without applying it to the actual tool.
